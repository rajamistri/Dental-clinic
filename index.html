For most adults a check-up and professional hygiene appointment every six months keeps the gums and enamel in good health. Patients with gum disease, implants, braces or a high risk of decay are often reviewed every three to four months we will agree the right interval for you at your first visit.

Yes — Dentéra welcomes new patients of all ages, including families. New patients receive a complimentary initial consultation with a full oral health assessment, digital X-rays where needed, and a written treatment plan with transparent pricing before anything begins.

A first consultation usually takes 40 to 45 minutes. That includes a conversation about your goals, a clinical examination, digital imaging and time to discuss your options. Follow-up review appointments are typically 20 to 30 minutes.

Absolutely. We offer porcelain veneers, composite bonding, professional whitening, gum contouring and complete smile makeovers. Every cosmetic case starts with a digital design so you can see a preview of your result, and we always prioritise conservative treatment that protects your natural teeth.

Implant placement is carried out under local anaesthetic, so you should not feel pain during the procedure — most patients describe it as easier than a tooth extraction. We use targeted, gentle anaesthesia and offer sedation options for anxious patients. Mild tenderness for a few days afterwards is normal and managed with standard pain relief.
